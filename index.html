PROGRAM

Coding centres teach you to write code. Schools teach you theory. But nowhere truly trains you how to become an owner.

In 12 weeks, you'll learn how to ideate, build, and operate a product through AI-agent systems. Learn how to own a start-up and find your very first paying customers.

01 · WK 1–3

Mindset & Idea

Start from a founder's mindset. You'll analyse and study real AI-agent products in the market. Interview customers yourself to gather your first insights.

✦ Validated wedge
02 · WK 4–6

Build the Agent

You'll learn how to set up AI agents yourself — Cursor, Claude, agent frameworks. From there, design and code the first steps of your product's V1.

✦ Live V1
03 · WK 7–9

Launch as a Startup

You'll learn how to price your product, set up payments, launch a brand, and handle the legal pieces. After launch day with your first customers, it's time to talk to them directly and gather real feedback.

✦ First $
04 · WK 10–12

Scale or Pivot

Learn to read your P&L to track revenue, costs, and net profit of your start-up. Plan the 90 days after the program to keep growing your company.

✦ Demo Day

HOW IT WORKS

Learn by doing. Ship something real from week one.

No long lectures. No pre-recorded videos. Every week you get one live session with Astro, a Codepet pair-programming with you 24/7, one real ship day, and a peer review with your cohort.

EVERY WEEK
Live with Astro
You'll meet and learn live with Astro — ask questions, review ideas, and discuss the product you're building together.
ALL THE TIME
Build with Codepet
Codepet is installed in your coding tools to support and work alongside you — nudging you whenever something's drifting off course.
EVERY FRIDAY
Deliver something new
You'll deliver a new feature to your product after each week of research and building.
WEEKENDS
Review together
Students get the chance to review each other's ideas and products — critique and add to one another's work to refine it together.

Astro Tran

With twenty years at top tech companies like Google and Meta, Astro also built and runs his own product, MURROR. He believes that in a world changing at dizzying speed, the fastest way to learn is to step into the real game as early as possible. Because when you fail early, you're still young enough to stand up and keep going.

A former member of Meta's NPE (New Product Experiment) team, Astro has always been first to put new ideas on the table. He believes that the intelligence and agility of the younger generation are what will carry the world much further.
